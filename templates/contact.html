Frequently Asked Questions

Is GreenHabit free to use?

Yes! GreenHabit is completely free. We believe in making eco-awareness accessible to everyone.

How often should I calculate my eco score?

We recommend daily tracking for the most accurate habit analysis, but you can use it as often as you'd like.

Can I save my previous scores?

Currently, scores are calculated in real-time and not stored. We're working on adding save features in future updates.

How accurate is the scoring system?

Our scoring is based on established environmental impact metrics and provides a good estimate of your daily eco-footprint.
