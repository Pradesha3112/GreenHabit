Our Methodology

Learn how we calculate your eco score and help you live more sustainably

Scoring System

Our eco score is calculated on a scale of 0-100, with 100 representing the most eco-friendly day possible.

Category Breakdown (25 points each):

Plastic Usage (25 points)

  • No plastic (25 pts): Used no single-use plastics
  • Low plastic (15 pts): Minimal plastic usage
  • High plastic (5 pts): Significant plastic usage
  • Used plastic (5 pts): Used plastic items

Transportation (25 points)

  • Walking (25 pts): Walked for all trips
  • Bicycle (25 pts): Used bicycle for transportation
  • Public transport (20 pts): Used buses, trains, or metro
  • Bike (10 pts): Used motorcycle or scooter
  • Car (5 pts): Used personal car

Food Choice (25 points)

  • Vegetarian (25 pts): Plant-based meals only
  • Mixed (15 pts): Combination of vegetarian and non-vegetarian
  • Non-vegetarian (5 pts): Meat or fish included in meals

Energy Usage (25 points)

  • Low (25 pts): Minimal energy consumption
  • Medium (15 pts): Moderate energy usage
  • High (5 pts): High energy consumption

Score Interpretation

80-100: Excellent

Outstanding eco-friendly habits! You're making a significant positive impact on the environment.

60-79: Moderate

Good effort with room for improvement. Small changes can significantly boost your score.

0-59: Needs Improvement

Opportunity to adopt more sustainable habits. Check our tips for easy improvements.

Personalized Tips Generation

Our system analyzes your lowest-scoring category to provide targeted recommendations:

1. Category Analysis

We identify which of the four categories has your lowest score.

2. Tip Selection

Based on the weakest category, we select the most relevant improvement tip.

3. Actionable Advice

You receive specific, practical advice to improve that particular habit.

Carbon Reduction

Higher scores correlate with lower carbon emissions

Waste Reduction

Better plastic habits mean less landfill waste

Resource Conservation

Lower energy usage preserves natural resources

Air Quality
